Comprehensive eye examinations

Canberra eye tests for clearer vision and earlier detection.

A thorough eye examination checks more than your glasses prescription. It assesses visual performance, eye health and risk factors that can affect long-term sight.

What is included

  • Vision and prescription assessment for glasses or contact lenses.
  • Eye pressure, eye movement and focusing checks.
  • Retinal and optic nerve assessment using diagnostic technology.
  • Screening for glaucoma, macular degeneration, diabetic eye disease and other ocular conditions.
  • A clear explanation of findings and recommended next steps.

Who should book

Book an eye test if you notice blurred vision, headaches, eye strain, difficulty reading, changes in night vision or if it has been more than two years since your last comprehensive examination. Patients with diabetes, family history of glaucoma or other risk factors may need more regular reviews.
