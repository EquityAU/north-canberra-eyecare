Children's vision assessments

Children's eye tests in Canberra for learning and confidence.

Vision plays a major role in learning, reading, sport and confidence. A child-friendly eye test checks clarity, focusing, eye teaming and ocular health.

Signs your child may need an eye test

  • Squinting, closing one eye or sitting close to screens.
  • Headaches, tiredness or losing place when reading.
  • Difficulty concentrating on near work.
  • One eye turning in or out.
  • Concerns raised by school, family history or failed screening.

Before school and beyond

A comprehensive eye examination is recommended before starting school, and sooner if there are concerns. Children may not know that their vision is different, so objective assessment matters.
