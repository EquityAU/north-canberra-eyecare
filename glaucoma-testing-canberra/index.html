Glaucoma screening

Glaucoma testing in Canberra with careful optic nerve assessment.

Glaucoma can progress quietly before vision changes are noticed. Regular testing helps detect risk earlier and supports timely referral or monitoring.

What we check

  • Eye pressure and corneal considerations.
  • Optic nerve appearance and structural risk signs.
  • Retinal imaging and comparison over time.
  • Visual function and symptoms where relevant.
  • Family history, age, medication and medical risk factors.

When to book

Book glaucoma testing if you have a family history of glaucoma, have been told your eye pressure is high, are over 40, have diabetes, use steroid medication or have noticed peripheral vision changes.
