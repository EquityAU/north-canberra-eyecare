About the practice

Patient-centred optometry with specialist-level perspective.

North Canberra Eyecare combines careful communication, modern diagnostic technology and long-term preventive care for patients across Canberra.

Phil Dang

Principal Optometrist, B.Optom(Hons) B.Sc UNSW

With over 15 years of experience spanning private practice and ophthalmic surgery environments, Phil offers a uniquely comprehensive approach to eye care.

He has worked alongside leading ophthalmologists across retinal, paediatric, cataract and refractive specialties, gaining extensive experience in complex ocular disease management, surgical co-management and advanced diagnostic imaging.

Beyond clinical practice, Phil has contributed to dry eye research, served on expert advisory panels for major therapeutic companies and teaches as a clinical educator at the University of Canberra.
