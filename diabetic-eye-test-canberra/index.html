Diabetic eye examinations

Diabetic eye tests in Canberra with careful retinal assessment.

Diabetes can affect the small blood vessels at the back of the eye. Regular retinal checks help identify changes early and support timely medical or specialist care.

Why diabetic eye checks matter

  • Diabetic retinopathy can develop before symptoms are obvious.
  • Retinal imaging can document changes and support monitoring.
  • Early detection helps protect long-term vision.
  • Reports and referrals can be arranged when further care is needed.

When to book

People living with diabetes should have regular eye examinations. The ideal review interval depends on your diabetes history, retinal findings and advice from your health care team.
